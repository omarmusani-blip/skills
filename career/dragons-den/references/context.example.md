# Context — your setup

Copy this to `references/context.md` (gitignored) and fill it in. The skill reads it before
every panel. Everything here is optional, but the more of it there is, the less the panel
has to guess.

## Where your evidence lives

- A list of what you've actually done (an experience bank, a master CV, a notes file):
  `path/to/experience.md`
- Where tailored CVs and essays live: `path/to/cvs/`
- Where you keep notes per application (requirements, open questions): `path/to/applications/`

## Standing known facts

Things every reviewer should know so they don't recommend the impossible:

- Citizenship / work rights, degree, year of study, availability dates
- Tools or skills you have *not* used (so nobody tells you to list them)
- Anything deliberately left off, and why

## How you want the report

- Tone, length, anything you've learned you don't want (e.g. "no bold claims")
- Any writing-voice or style guide the final text should follow
